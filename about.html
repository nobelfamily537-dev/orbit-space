About Orbit

Orbit is a small space-science site. The idea is simple: space news is often written for people who already know the subject. We write it for everyone else.

Every article here is available in English and Hindi. We do not use machine translation on the page — each language is written and checked separately.

Where our information comes from

We use public information from space agencies and reference sources. Mission names, dates and figures are taken from official sources such as ISRO and NASA, and from public mission records.

Images

All images on this site come from public-domain sources, mainly NASA and Wikimedia Commons. They are free to use and credited where required.

Corrections

If you spot a mistake, write to us. We fix errors quickly and note the date of every update.
